## 2. Who can use the app

- You must be **18 or older**.
- You must be a current student at an approved university and sign up with your own university email.
- One person, one account. A verified email gives you one account with one known profile and, if you choose, one anonymous profile.
- You must re-verify your email once per academic year (see section 11).

## 3. Your profiles

- Your **known profile** shows your real name.
- Your **anonymous profile** hides your identity from other students. You can create it once your account is a few days old and has no warnings against it. You will be shown a plain explanation of what anonymous means here and asked to confirm you have read it.
- We keep an encrypted link between your account and your anonymous profile. We can use it only in the limited cases described in section 7.
- Choose carefully which profile you post from. The app shows you which profile you are using and asks you to confirm your first few anonymous posts, but you are responsible for what you post.

## 5. What is not allowed

See the Community Rules. In short: no threats, harassment or bullying; no targeting named individuals in anonymous posts; no sharing others' private details or photos without consent; no non-consensual sexual content; no hate speech; no impersonation; no attempts to unmask anonymous profiles or to get around bans.

## 6. Moderation and enforcement

- Anyone can report a post, comment or profile. A post or comment reported by **3 or more different students** is hidden until a moderator reviews it. If you have two profiles, your reports count as one person.
- Reports about threats, self-harm and intimate images are reviewed first.
- We may remove content and apply:
  - a **warning**, which also stops you creating an anonymous profile which stops counting after 12 months [controller and counsel to confirm];
  - a **temporary suspension** of up to 30 days, during which you cannot sign in;
  - a **permanent ban**.
- **Penalties apply to the verified account, not the profile.** A ban hides both profiles and their content and blocks that university email from signing up again.
- **You can appeal a ban once.** You do this by requesting a code to your university email and sending a short explanation. If the appeal succeeds, your account, profile and the content the ban hid are restored. Content you deleted yourself, or that was removed for another reason, is not.
- Decisions are made by people. Our automated check (section 8) only brings a post to a moderator's attention.

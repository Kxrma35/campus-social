# Community Rules

> Shown, in short form, at sign-up. Plain language on purpose. Draft v0.2 for review.

This is a space for students. Everyone here is a real, verified student. Anonymous doesn't mean accountable to nobody: if you break these rules, the penalty falls on your verified account, whichever profile you used.

## The rules

1. **No threats, harassment or bullying.** Don't intimidate, insult or pile on anyone.
2. **Don't name, describe or target a specific person in anonymous posts.** If you wouldn't say it under your own name, don't say it about a person under a mask.
3. **Don't share someone's photos, contacts or private details without their consent.**
4. **No sexual content involving anyone who hasn't consented.** This includes intimate images shared without permission.
5. **No hate speech against any group.**
6. **No impersonating others, and no trying to work out who is behind an anonymous profile.**
7. **No evading bans or creating extra accounts.**

## Reporting

Every post, comment and profile has a **Report** button. If 3 or more different students report a post or comment, it is hidden until a moderator checks it. Reports about threats, self-harm and intimate images are looked at first.

Your report is never shown to the person you reported, and moderators cannot see who made it.

## What happens if rules are broken

A moderator can give a **warning**, a **temporary suspension** (up to 30 days) or a **permanent ban**. These apply to your account, so both of your profiles. A warning also stops you creating an anonymous profile, and stops counting after 12 months.

You can **appeal a ban once**, using a code sent to your university email.

## Anonymous profiles

You can create an anonymous profile once your account is a few days old and has no warnings against it. Moderators can act on anonymous posts without knowing who wrote them.

## When we can see who you are

Other students can't see who owns an anonymous profile. We can, but only for serious rule-breaking (threats, targeted harassment, intimate images without consent, exposing private details) or a valid legal order. A very small group of named staff can do this, a second person must approve it unless it is a court order, and every lookup is recorded.

We can't stop someone recognising your writing, or taking a screenshot. Think before you post.

## If you or someone you know is struggling

Posts that may suggest self-harm are automatically brought to a moderator's attention, and you'll be shown support information. This is not perfect, so please reach out to someone directly too.

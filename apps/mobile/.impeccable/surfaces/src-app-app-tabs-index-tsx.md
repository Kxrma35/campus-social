# Moku: whole app

Scope and visitor mode: the whole mobile app (sign in, feed, post, composer, profiles, notifications, settings). Operate: students complete small social tasks between classes on phones.

Audience, job, constraints: verified USIU-Africa students post, read, react, report. Colour code is binding (existing blue, lilac for anonymous, neutrals). Identity labels stay as text. Honest-anonymity copy and safety flows are unchanged. 44 pt targets, WCAG AA, dark and light.

Pinned by the user: "like Instagram and Substack mashed together". Chosen elements: photos lead the post, person-centred header, a rail of people you follow, tactile reactions, reading-first typography, a serif reading face, a quiet text-led list, a writing prompt at the top.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A campus feed that reads like a small publication with a face. Every post opens with a person and is set like a short published note; photos run edge to edge. It refuses the boxed-card list of identical outlined buttons.

OWN-WORLD: Existing neutrals and blue, lilac for anonymous, unchanged. Literata for post and writing text, system sans for every control and label. Hairlines derived from the existing border colour, round avatars, line icons drawn at one stroke weight. Anonymous is a lilac disc with the overlapping-circles mark; known is a blue-tinted disc with the initial.

STORY: A student opens the app, sees who is posting, reads without effort, reacts with a satisfying tap, and writes in a calm space where which identity is speaking is always visible.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar "Feed". A writing prompt row (your avatar, "What is on your mind?", image icon). A rail of avatars of people you follow. The three-segment control (Newest, Top this week, Following). Then the first post: avatar, name, handle and time, identity chip, serif text, full-bleed photo, icon action row (heart count, comment count, More).

FORM: user-pinned (no roll, seed key: none). Signature move: the two-circle identity mark as avatar and as the composer's posting-as switch.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

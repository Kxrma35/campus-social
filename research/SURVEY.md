# USIU-Africa Student Survey

Status: draft, 2026-10-12. Purpose: quantify what interviews suggest (build plan milestone M6).
Target: 100+ responses from USIU-Africa students aged 18 or older. Estimated time: 4 minutes.
Build in a form tool you control. Check its data location and sharing settings before use.

## Intro screen (shown before any question)

> **Student social media survey**
> I'm researching how USIU-Africa students use social media, for an early-stage project. It takes about 4 minutes.
>
> - You must be 18 or older and a current USIU-Africa student.
> - It is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
> - Answers are anonymous. We do not ask for your name, email or student number.
> - Results will be reported only as totals and general themes.
> - The data is stored in [tool / location: fill in] and will be deleted by [date: fill in].
> - Contact: [your contact: fill in]
>
> [ ] I am 18 or older and a current USIU-Africa student, and I agree to take part. (required, unticked by default)

If the box is not ticked, end the survey.

## Section 1: About you
1. Year of study: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / Postgraduate / Other
2. Faculty or school (optional dropdown)

No gender, age, or other demographic questions unless needed later.

## Section 2: Current social media use
3. Which do you use at least weekly? *(select all)* Instagram / TikTok / X / Snapchat / WhatsApp Status / Facebook / YouTube / Other: ___
4. How often do you post (not just scroll) on your main platform?
   Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Rarely / Never
5. Compared with a year ago, do you post: More / About the same / Less
6. If you post less, why? *(select all, optional)*
   - Family or relatives follow me
   - Lecturers or employers might see
   - Worried about judgement from classmates
   - Don't want a permanent record
   - Lost interest
   - Other: ___

## Section 3: Audience and self-censorship
7. How much do you agree? (Strongly disagree 1 to Strongly agree 5)
   a. I have wanted to post something but didn't because of who might see it.
   b. Family members follow me on the platform I use most.
   c. I would post more if only students at my campus could see my posts.
   d. I would post more if I could post without my name attached.
8. Do you have a second or private account on any platform? Yes / No / Prefer not to say

## Section 4: Concept reaction

Show this short description:
> *An app that only verified students at your campus can see. You sign up with your university email. You have a normal profile with your real name, and you can add a separate anonymous profile. You choose, post by post, which one to speak as. Other students can't see who is behind an anonymous profile. The company can, if someone seriously breaks the rules or the law requires it.*

9. How likely are you to join? (1 very unlikely to 5 very likely)
10. If you joined, which would you use more?
    Known profile / Anonymous profile / Both equally / Neither
11. How likely are you to post, not only read? (1 to 5)
12. What would you most likely use it for? *(select up to 2)*
    Campus news and events / Jokes and memes / Opinions and discussion / Asking for advice / Venting / Sharing photos / Other: ___
13. What would worry you most? *(select up to 2)*
    Bullying or harassment / Being identified / Gossip about people / Privacy of my data / The company seeing who I am / Being too empty / Other: ___

## Section 5: Trust
14. How important is each for you to trust the app? (Not important 1 to Essential 5)
    a. Fast removal of threats and harassment
    b. Clear rules shown at sign-up
    c. Student moderators
    d. University staff having no access
    e. Seeing how reports are handled
    f. Being able to delete my data
15. Who, if anyone, should be able to find out who owns an anonymous profile?
    *(select all)* Nobody / Only if required by a court order / Also for serious threats / Also for university disciplinary cases / Not sure

## Section 6: Optional follow-up
16. Open question (optional): What would make you not use an app like this?
17. Would you like to test an early version? If yes, enter an email: ___
    *(Store this separately from survey answers. Use only to contact about testing, and say so on the screen.)*

## Analysis plan

| Question | Reading |
|---|---|
| Q5, Q7a | Share who post less or self-censor because of audience: confirms the core problem |
| Q7b, Q7c, Q7d | Is the audience limit (c) or anonymity (d) the stronger driver? |
| Q9, Q11 | Intent to join vs intent to post; posting is the key one |
| Q10 | Known vs anonymous split to expect |
| Q13, Q14, Q15 | Moderation priorities and the unmask policy decision |

Report totals and percentages; do not claim more than a convenience sample supports. Respondents who are friends of the founders will skew results; note recruitment channels per response if you can do so without identifying people.

## Distribution
- Share through class groups, clubs, hostels and ambassadors, with the same intro text.
- Do not offer incentives that depend on completing particular answers.
- Do not post it anywhere that would reach people under 18.
